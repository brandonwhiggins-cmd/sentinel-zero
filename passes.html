Your subscription only initiates once our 500 verified Early Adopter threshold is achieved and our CS2 & R6 Siege matchmaking servers officially go live. What are the daily Primetime Matchmaking Hours?

To guarantee rapid queue times (<60 seconds) without empty lobbies, matchmaking runs daily during peak hours: 5:00 PM – 10:30 PM CT (6:00 PM – 11:30 PM ET / 3:00 PM – 8:30 PM PT). Centering in US-Central gives optimal ping balance across North American players.

Which games are playable at launch?

Counter-Strike 2 and Rainbow Six Siege are locked as our 2 Day-1 launch titles. Every Early Adopter receives a vote to decide which of the 14 Season 1 titles (Warzone, VALORANT, WARDOGS, Apex, Tarkov Arena, etc.) unlocks next.

Does the pass work on Console (PS5 / Xbox)?

No. All Sentinel Zero passes and tournaments are strictly for Windows PC (Windows 10 / 11 64-bit). You must possess a compatible PC capable of running SentinelAgent.exe to enter competitive lobbies.

Can I cancel my pass at any time?

Yes, absolutely. All passes are month-to-month with zero lock-in contracts. You can cancel with 1-click in your Stripe customer portal anytime.
