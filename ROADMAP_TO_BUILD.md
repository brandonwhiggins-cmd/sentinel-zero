## 🎯 High-Priority Backlog (Next Feature Sprints)

### 1. 👥 Universal Creator & Squad Referral Engine ("Every Player is an Affiliate")
> **Core Concept:** Do not gate creator codes to only large Twitch streamers. Give *every single player* a custom referral code/link upon account creation so casual squad leaders can recruit their friends and play for free.

* [ ] **Dynamic Referral Links & Codes:**
  * Auto-generate a custom referral link (`https://www.sentinelzero.gg/?ref=username`) and promo code (`USERNAME`) for every registered profile.
* [ ] **The "Refer 4, Yours is Free" Revenue Share (STRICT PAID CONVERSION RULE):**
  * **Paid Subscribers Only:** A referral is **NOT credited** merely for sending a link or having a friend sign up. The referred friend **must complete payment and become an active paying subscriber** before any commission or offset is issued.
  * **25% Recurring Monthly Split:** Referrer earns ~$1.75/month for every active, paying Pro Pass subscriber ($6.99/mo) and ~$3.25/month for paying Elite Pass subscribers ($12.99/mo).
  * **Squad Leader Offset:** When 4 referred squadmates maintain active paid subscriptions, the cumulative $7.00/mo credit offsets the squad leader's own monthly subscription in full.
  * **Cancellation Rule:** If a referred friend cancels their paid subscription, the commission and offset for that seat immediately ceases.
* [ ] **Optional 2-Tier "Squad Tree" Multiplier:**
  * **Tier 1 (Direct Friends):** 20% recurring from active paid accounts.
  * **Tier 2 (Friends of Friends):** 5% recurring from active paid accounts.
  * Strictly capped at 2 tiers to maintain esports credibility and avoid MLM aesthetics.
* [ ] **In-Platform Escrow Wallet Payouts:**
  * Referral earnings credit directly to the user's Sentinel Zero Escrow Wallet.
  * Gamers can use credits immediately to enter cash duels and tournaments, driving match liquidity.
  * Cash-out threshold set at $20.00 or $50.00 via Stripe Connect.
* [ ] **Hardware-Bound Anti-Fraud Protections:**
  * **Household Allowed:** Up to 3–4 accounts can share the same family credit card (siblings, couples, roommates).
  * **Hardware TPM Lock:** Multiple accounts created on the exact same motherboard/TPM silicon cannot refer each other.
  * **No Self-Referrals:** System verifies distinct hardware hashes and distinct payment instruments.
* [ ] **In-App "Squad & Creator Hub" Tab:**
  * Clean dashboard showing: Friends Invited, Active Paid Squad Subscribers, Monthly Earnings, and 1-Click Wallet Claim.
