Hybrid Anti-Cheat Architecture & Threat Model

A technically honest breakdown of how Sentinel Zero prevents cheating without invasive kernel rootkits.

01 Layer 1: Pre-Launch Silicon Hardware Attestation

Before matches launch, SentinelAgent.exe queries hardware TPM 2.0 PCR registers to inspect the UEFI boot path, Secure Boot state, and active hypervisor invariant timing. Crucially, it validates IOMMU (VT-d / AMD-Vi) DMA hardware isolation, blocking rogue PCIe FPGA Direct Memory Access cards from reading physical RAM addresses before the game process executes.

02 Layer 2: Real-Time Biometric Input Guarding

Unlike spyware that scans personal browser cookies, Sentinel Zero analyzes microsecond HID input streams. Mouse jerk entropy, acceleration derivative curves, and micro-timing jitters are fed through real-time statistical filters. Zero-variance recoil macro scripts (Cronus Zen, Arduino USB spoofers) and unnatural snap aimbots are flagged in < 4ms based on mathematical impossibilities rather than invasive file inspections.

03 Layer 3: Economic Disincentive & Escrow Shield

In traditional games, cheaters risk a $15 burner account. In Sentinel Zero, all matches operate under enforceable legal contracts: cheaters face 100% immediate forfeiture of their match stake as pre-agreed liquidated damages under Section 4 of our ToS.
